Dayglint

One line a day

Dayglint is a journal that asks for one line a day. Pick a mood, write a sentence, add a photo if there was one worth keeping. Look back through a mood-tinted calendar, "on this day" memories and a weekly recap.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: write to contact@bible-habit.com. We read every message.

Subscriptions

Dayglint Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. You can cancel at any time in your store account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
